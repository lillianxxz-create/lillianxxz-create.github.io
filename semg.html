Ballet Injury Prevention with Surface Electromyography (sEMG): Muscle Control Monitoring

sEMG Sensor Waistband for Visualizing Muscle Activation Abstract

Abstract

This project aims to (1) quantify muscle activation patterns during foundational technique, (2) detect deviation from safe, standard execution in real time, and (3) provide low-burden feedback that helps dancers self-correct before overexertion occurs. The specific objective is to integrate surface electromyography (sEMG) from key muscle groups with a computer vision model that classifies movement quality compared to professionally evaluated exemplars.
